About Me ♀

Welcome to my about page. I hope you enjoy getting to know me better!

Hi, I'm Stephanie,

Thank you for visiting my website. Becoming a Web Developer and Graphic Designer was a big career change for me after 22 years of working in the industry of Tourism and Hospitality Management. Luckily for me I embrace change and remind myself every day that I don't want to die in the future without living in today. To get to know more about me click the decription boxes below...

Get to Know Me Better...

I grew up on small, island community off the south coast of my island province of Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada. The fishing industry was a very important part of everyone's lives along with hunting. I often lobster potted, squid jigged and fished with my father. I did not hunt but often had wild game for meals if it wasn't seafood. We had our own garden with a variety of home grown vegetables that we used until the following year's harvest.

I had a very simple childhood filled with love, laughter and happiness. I was wealthy in a very different way than most people would percieve having wealth to be. The ocean and all it's special gifts, my grandparents, my aunts and uncles, friends, and of course my parents were my entire world.I could not have asked for a better childhood and it certainly helped shape the woman I grew into.

I always enoyed school and learning as a child that continued into my adulthood. My highschool granted me an entrance scholarship to my University/College of choice upon graduation which started my journey into the big, wide world. Some of my education includes.

  • Memorial University(St. John's, NL, Canada) - Pre-Bachelor Studies in Science
  • College of the North Atlantic(Affiliate of Memorial Univeristy, St.John's Canada) - Tourism and Hospitality Management
  • British Columbia Institute of Technology(British Columbia, Canada) - Front End Web Deleopment and Graphic Design

Throughout my studies I became involved in the hospitality industry which helped pay for my schooling, travel, and living expenses. Eventually I entered into supervision, management, and other leadership roles. Jobs were always plentiful in the service industry so ultimately it's where I stayed and moved up in my positions. Over the years I trained in other areas to explore my options including:

  • Bookkeeping
  • Secretarial
  • Magazine Editing
  • Construction and Renovations
  • Benefits Administrator (LLQP Certified)

Travel has been a fascination of mine since a young child. I was often exposed to programs such as National Geographic, History Channel, and Discovery channel heightening my passion for things like travel, space exploration, ancient cultures and civilizations, nature and wildlife, and photography. Traveling to western Canada in my early 20's gave me access to the Rocky Mountains and that's when I fell in love with hiking. Since then I've hiked mountains in Asia, North America, and South America while currently planning for Africa, Europe, and New Zealand. Dian Fossey has been a heroine of mine since a child and peaked my interest in conservation and wildlife preservation. My acceptance into a habitat and wildlife conservation program on Madagascar Island in Africa will be one of the major highlights of my Africa trip. My roots will always bring me back to the love of the aquatic world - my truest happy place - where I will continue to love fishing, kayaking, iceberg and whale watching, swimming, and boating.
Becoming a skilled, professional Web Developer and Graphic Designer while working remotely is my long term goal. This industry is the opportunity of a lifetime to live my life doing all the things I love while helping companies, businesses, organizations and entrepreneurs build beautiful websites from the comfort of wherever I live or travel to. Being able give back to the world helping worthy causes and organizations is also very important to me so designing or re-creating websites for such cause would also be a very important goal for me to achieve.
